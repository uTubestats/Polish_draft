Privacy Policy

Your writing

When you press Polish, the text, your selected options, and any context or custom instructions are sent to an AI model through the Puter service to produce the result. Your text is sent directly from your browser to Puter (puter.com), which runs the model and may ask you to sign in to a free Puter account; Puter’s privacy policy applies. PolishDraft’s own code does not store or log your text. The AI provider and the hosting platform may process or retain data under their own policies; we do not control that, so avoid pasting passwords, financial details or other sensitive information.

Contact messages

The contact form asks only for your name, email address, a subject and your message, used solely to reply to you. The form opens your own email app with the message filled in; nothing is sent until you press send there, and it then passes through your email provider and is kept in the inboxes involved. We do not use contact details for marketing. Ask us to delete a message by emailing us.

Fonts

The site loads its fonts (Fraunces and Plus Jakarta Sans) from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google servers and your IP address is visible to them.

Cookies and storage

Our own code uses your browser’s local storage only to remember your light or dark theme. We set no advertising or tracking cookies and run no analytics. When you use the AI tool, the Puter script is loaded and may use its own storage, for example to keep you signed in, under Puter’s policies.

Advertising

No ads are active. If advertising is added later, this policy and a consent mechanism must be updated first.

Your choices

You can clear the editor at any time and reset the theme by clearing site data in your browser.
