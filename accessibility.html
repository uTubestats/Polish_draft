Accessibility Statement

We aim for a site that works with keyboard and screen readers: semantic headings, labelled form fields, visible focus outlines, text-based error messages, sufficient contrast, and support for reduced-motion and dark-mode preferences. We have not completed a formal audit, so some issues may remain.

To report a problem, use the email on the Contact page.
